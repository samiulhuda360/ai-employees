# Fernway product dossier

Fernway is a **fictional company** used to demonstrate the fleet. This dossier is what Growth
Scout reads to avoid recommending things that already exist.

## What Fernway does today

| Feature | Plan | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free Google Business Profile check | Free | Scores a profile out of 100 and lists the top fixes |
| Review requests by SMS and email | Starter | Sends after a job is marked done; one reminder after 3 days |
| Review reply suggestions | Starter | Drafts a reply for each new review; the owner edits and posts it |
| Local rank tracking | Pro | Up to 10 keywords, checked weekly from the business's address |
| Monthly PDF report | Pro | Reviews, rating trend and rank changes, branded for agencies |

## Who pays

- Owner-operated local service businesses (salons, dentists, trades) on Starter.
- Small marketing agencies on Pro, managing 5-30 client locations.

## Known gaps (customers ask for these)

- Competitor review tracking.
- A website widget that shows recent reviews.
- Multi-location roll-up for agencies.

## Constraints

- One developer; anything taking more than two weeks needs a strong case.
- No automated posting to Google on a customer's behalf.
